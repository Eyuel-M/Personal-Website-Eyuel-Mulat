Client Nexus Technologies
Year 2024
Scope Brand Identity · UI Design
Role Creative Director
01 / Overview

Data at scale. Clarity by design.

Nexus Technologies needed an interface that could handle enterprise-scale data while remaining immediately legible to any user. The challenge: complexity without confusion.

We built a visual language rooted in spatial hierarchy — clear typographic scales, a restrained color palette, and a grid system precise enough to hold hundreds of data points without visual noise. The brand identity extended from the UI, giving Nexus a cohesive presence across product and marketing.
