Identity System 2023

Vanguard
Capital

Client Vanguard Capital Group
Year 2023
Scope Identity · Strategy · Print
Role Brand Director
01 / Overview

Authority built from the ground up.

Vanguard Capital was entering a crowded market with a differentiated thesis but a brand that didn't reflect it. They needed an identity that projected the same structural confidence as their investment philosophy.

We built a system grounded in architectural geometry — a mark that works at billboard scale and business card scale, a typographic hierarchy that commands trust, and a color vocabulary that signals precision without coldness. The result is an identity that performs across every medium the firm operates in.

02 / Deliverables
01

Logo System

Primary mark, wordmark, and responsive lockups for all scales from digital favicon to exterior signage.

02

Brand Standards

Comprehensive brand guidelines covering color, typography, photography style, and usage rules.

03

Collateral Suite

Business cards, letterhead, pitch deck template, and investor report design system.
